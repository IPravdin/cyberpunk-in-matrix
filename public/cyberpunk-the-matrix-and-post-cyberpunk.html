Main Characteristics of

Cyberpunk

Post-Cyberpunk

And The Matrix (1999)

1

Lenses

Cyberpunk and Post-Cyberpunk are lenses which allow to re-understand past and present in our world respectively (Kilgore 2020, 48-55).
 
The authors are using nowadays technological, economic, cultural and social tendencies to project their development on the future and demonstrate their effects.

The Matrix (1999) predicts future where AI via VR - new technologies for the late 90s - will control the World.

2

Style

Tech-noir is a main aesthetic style for the Cyberpunk genre (Auger 2011). 
Post-cyberpunk is a constant experiment from authors and their imagination
​(Kilgore 2020, 48-55). 
The Matrix (1999) ​uses different technical and aesthetical tools to present the Virtual and the Real worlds. 
​And invents new rules in the worlds:

Mobile phone and telephone are communication mediums between the Matrix and the Reality for the protagonists.


​
​Also, a telephone is an exit from the Matrix.

3

Hacker


​is a cliche protagonist (for Cyberpunk and Post-Cyberpunk)



​who fights against the “norms” and the system.

4

World Control

In Cyberpunk and Post-Cyberpunk,
​Transnational Corporations wield tremendous power over governments. And all wealth belongs only to several people, while mass live below the poverty line.
In The Matrix (1999) Artificial Intelligence (AI) controls the Real and Virtual worlds via substitution of the Reality by the Virtuality.

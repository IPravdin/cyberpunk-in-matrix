How are the social and philosophical influences in The Matrix in late 90's? 
  1. Plato's allegory of cave.
    In the movie we see that people in The Matrix (1999) believe what they are shown by the matrix just like the people in the cave.
  2. Two groups shown one knows about reality and other doesn't. This can be seen as elitism. 
  3. We see religious philosophy in The Matrix (1999).
    When Neo wakes up liquid-filled chamber(real world), then the entire sequence of him falling to water and then being picked up can been seen as reference to baptism. We also see Neo coming back to life after dying near the end of the movie.
  4. Questioning reality and perception. 
    We see in the movie Neo constantly doubts the reality. This is depicted in Neo's dialogues, for the first 45 minutes in the movie neo asks 44 questions, nearly one question per minute. Later in the movie we see Morpheus explaining senses can mislead us. 
  5. Rock culture and all the metal music is present in the movie. The club where Neo meets Trinity first time the music is played behind is metal. We see black leather clothing inspired by 90 metal musicians.  
  6. Postmodernism has heavily influenced the movie.

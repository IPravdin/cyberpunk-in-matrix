What is reality?
Jean Baudrillard. "Simulacrum and Simulation" 
Jean’s references in the movie
  1. "Rather than merely predicting a future dystopia, Baudrillard’s work argues that dystopia had already been realized at the time of writing"  (Haar, Rebecca,  and McFarlane 2020, 256). 
  2. "He coins the term ‘hyperreality’ to describe this society in which simulacra shape the human life world and a pretense at a reference to reality is no longer required" (Haar, Rebecca, and McFarlane 2020, 256). 
  3. We see Jean's book Simulation and Simulacra in the movie which is hollowed out after the chapter “On Nihilism”. This chapter at the end of the book whereas in the book that's shown in the movie has it in the middle. This chapter  in the book indicates that, the Universe and all of us, have entered live into simulation.  (Jonas Čeika - CCK Philosophy, "What did Baudrillard think about The Matrix?". YouTube video)
  4. "The association between cash, information, and simulation set up by this Baudrillardian intertextuality suggests the circulation of images in a highly mediated society that results in the emptying-out of meaning, like the emptying-out of the hollow book" ​(Haar, Rebecca, and McFarlane 2020, 256).
  5. “Welcome to the desert of the real” is a quote from the Jean's book "Simulation and Simulacra". If everything is simulation and looks, feels and behaves more realistic than the real “Hyperreal” then reality becomes a desert that no one wants to live in.  (Jonas Čeika - CCK Philosophy, "What did Baudrillard think about The Matrix?". YouTube video)

His Distancing from The Matrix
  1. "In the matrix, the line between simulated world and real world is clear. It’s this distinction that Jean believes we cannot make, there’s never any certainty about if someone’s in matrix or not, what they are experiencing is simulated or not"(Jonas Čeika - CCK Philosophy, "What did Baudrillard think about The Matrix?". YouTube video). 
  2. "We can't distinguish between the map and the Territory between the simulated and the real between the copy and the original between the cave and the outside. We can't escape the simulation because we wouldn't know what to escape from in the first place" (Jonas Čeika - CCK Philosophy, "What did Baudrillard think about The Matrix?". YouTube video).

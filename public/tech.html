How did the Matrix changed Sci-Fi action movies? 
​
  1. The Matrix (1999) added level of complexity to a sci-fi action movie, which defined a new standard for the future movies.
  2. "Bullet time" the iconic visual effect created in the Matrix (1999), which allows the viewer to explore a moment progressing in slow-motion as the camera appears to orbit around the scene at normal speed.   
  3. "Stylistic way of showing you’re in constructed reality where time and space are not the same"(Flashback FilmMaking, "What is Bullet Time 'The Matrix' Behind The Scenes". YouTube Video). 
  4. Matrix also shown the "time-slice" effect with completely frozen characters and surroundings. 
  5. Orbital rig consisted of 120 camera’s 2 motion cameras  ​
  6. Max Payne used the same bullet time mechanics.
